Questions

Why does my phone say it's unsafe?

Google warns about any app downloaded outside the Play Store that uses Accessibility. Promise Keeper needs Accessibility to see the WhatsApp messages you send. The warning is about that permission, not about anything the app does.

Can it send my messages anywhere?

No. The app doesn't have Android's internet permission, so Android itself blocks it from going online. It only looks at WhatsApp, only at messages you send, and everything is processed on your phone.

My bank or UPI app won't open

Some payment apps refuse to run while an Accessibility app from outside the Play Store is on. Before paying, open Promise Keeper → Settings → Turn off for now. Turn it back on afterwards from step 2 of the same screen.

How do I update?

Download and install the new version from this page the same way. It installs over the old one and keeps your reminders.

Is the code public?

Yes, all of it is on GitHub, where you can also check each release's SHA-256.
